# Icon artwork

The following library artwork is reused in the app's existing 24-unit, rounded-stroke icon renderer. Paths are unchanged, and food icons retain the app's 1.8 stroke weight.

- Chicken: [Lucide Drumstick](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/drumstick.svg).
- Ice cream: [Lucide Ice Cream Cone](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/ice-cream-cone.svg).
- Empty photo slots: [Lucide Camera](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/icons/camera.svg), including its circle element.
- Turkey: [Tabler Meat](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/icons/outline/meat.svg).
- Yogurt, sour cream and pudding: [Tabler Bowl Spoon](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/main/icons/outline/bowl-spoon.svg).

Retrieved 2026-10-13. Licenses are included in [lucide-LICENSE](lucide-LICENSE) and [tabler-LICENSE](tabler-LICENSE). Icons inherit text color, remain decorative beside visible labels, and do not require an external runtime or network request.
